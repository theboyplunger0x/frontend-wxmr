Know the
difference.

Does wXMR have Monero's privacy? +

No. Solana balances and transfers are public. Public bridge records can associate wallets, Monero addresses, amounts, and timing. wXMR does not bring Monero's native transaction privacy to Solana.

What am I trusting? +

You depend on the bridge operator holding the XMR reserves and processing redemptions. Software, upgrade authorities, network availability, and liquidity also introduce risks. Reserve proofs help you inspect evidence; they do not remove these dependencies.

Is 1 wXMR always worth 1 XMR? +

The bridge accounts for issuance and redemption at a 1:1 unit ratio before fees. Market prices can differ, and redemptions depend on the bridge processing your request.

What does it cost, and how long does it take? +

The reference bridge charges 0.1% each way, with a minimum of 0.1 XMR. Network costs are separate. Deposits require 10 Monero confirmations before bridge processing. Confirmation and processing times vary; consult the official app for current conditions.

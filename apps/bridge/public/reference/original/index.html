What does a proof tell you?

A reserve proof supports checking control of the included Monero outputs and their spent status. It does not guarantee future reserves or redemption availability. Operational balances and proof snapshots can refer to different times.

A LITTLE CONTEXT GOES A LONG WAY

Two networks.
A clear path.

XMR is native Monero. wXMR represents XMR held by the bridge, on Solana.

01

Connect & get an address.

Use a Solana wallet to create your reusable Monero deposit address. You'll need SOL for network fees and account creation.

02

Send native XMR.

Send at least 0.1 XMR. The bridge begins processing after 10 Monero confirmations; timing depends on block production.

03

Receive wXMR.

A 1 XMR deposit results in 0.999 wXMR for you, with 0.001 wXMR charged as the bridge fee.

How do I return to Monero? −

Choose To Monero, enter the amount and your Monero destination, then review and sign the withdrawal request. The operator processes the native XMR payment. Eligible pending withdrawals may be cancelled after the on-chain waiting period; the original bridge fee is retained.

What should I know about privacy and custody? +

Solana balances and transfers are public. Bridge records can associate addresses, amounts and timing. wXMR depends on the bridge operator holding reserves and processing redemptions, as well as software and network availability.
